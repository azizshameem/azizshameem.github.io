Why I'm Writing

Created in January 01, 2023

2023   ·   blog,   content

Hello Readers! This is my first post on this blog.
I intend for this to be a space where I document any and all of my thoughts : academic, personal (in experiences, learnings and such), philosophical/psychological, pedagogical or a coherent amalgam thereof. I want to use this place to share any life events that have helped shape me in various ways, in the hope that it might provide guidance to people treading similar paths in the future, or be a fun read for my contemporaries who themselves have undergone similar transformations and can therefore relate. I might also include thoughts on tech/non-tech concepts (particular in the ML space) that I find intriguing, in an effort to try and elucidate them in my own imperfect way.

By its nature, the posts I put here are supposed to be a collections of my personal opinions and therefore, biased, and I intend to keep it that way. Any views that I descirbe here are, therefore, my own and in no way claim to represent any consensus or influence. All content posted here will be in my own language purely, without interference from any LLM or other refinement tools. As an overthinker myself, I realize that I have some thoughts and opinions on several things that I encounter on a day-to-day basis. This is why I took to journaling as a method to jot some of them down and revisit them at a later date, either to revise or to add to them as a more mature version of myself. I think it is time now that some of them see the light of public face, atleast the ones that I feel confident sharing.

I hope that, through this endeavour, I will not only be able to inspire, motivate, console, direct, teach or just make aware people that find themselves stuck in thorns as they tread this forest of life, or just want a good read on random topics, but also be able to structure my own thoughts better and be less affected by them.

Any feedbacks and/or comments on any of my writing are always welcome here. Do let me know if you find anything even mildly interesting or otherwise, or have any different views on this or related topics and would like to discuss (I surely would), it will help me structure future posts and/or may lead to some insightful conversations.

Cheers !
This is me,
An Aspirant
