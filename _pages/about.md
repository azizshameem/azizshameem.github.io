Hello, and welcome to my personal space on the web!    
I am final-year student at IIT Bombay, a researcher and enthusiast in Data Science and Machine Learning, blending technical expertise with a drive for innovation. My interests primarily include Generative Machine Learning and its applications, although I separately enjoy sub fields like Optimization, Deep Learning Fundamentals (interpretability, trainability and the like) and Control Systems.  
Find details about my work and projects in my [CV/Résumé](assets/pdf/CurriculumVitae.pdf). You can also find it in the [CV](/cv/) section. 

I currently reside at IIT Bombay, though I hail from Pune, where I have attended my secondary and high school. I can speak English and Hindi(and Urdu, somewhat) fluently.  

When I’m not immersed in code or datasets, you might find me serving on a tennis court, strategizing over a game of chess, enjoying a cricket match, or unwinding with my guitar or drums

In my free time, I:
* Like to go through the recent developements in Artifical Intelligence and Machine Learning (or divulge in random discussions thereof)
* Surf youtube for tutorials or walk throughs on things I find interesting
* Have in depth conversations with people about life and its aspects : things that matter
* Watch light-hearted to dark comedy
* Hang out with friends

I am inclined towards calm and meaningful music, usually those associated with some events in my past. I like the nostalgia it brings with it. I have not considered having a favorite artist/band, but I usually end up listening to older songs, or ones that resonate with me.  

I have been trying to maintain a [blog](/blog/) where I can jot down both academic and non-academic articles/experiences that I find worthwhile, although I have not gotten around adding anything recently. I hope to add several new posts to it in the coming months.  

Feel free to reach out if you have something interesting to say, or just say hi !
